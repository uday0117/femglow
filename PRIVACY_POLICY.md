# Privacy Policy for FemGlow

**Last Updated: May 14, 2026**

## Introduction

UK Solutions ("us", "we", or "our") operates the FemGlow mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect any personal information.**

FemGlow is a period tracking and women's health application that operates entirely locally on your device. We do not:

- Collect personal information
- Track your usage
- Store your health data on our servers
- Transmit data to external servers
- Use cookies or tracking technologies
- Require account registration or login
- Share your data with third parties
- Request unnecessary permissions

## Data Storage

All period tracking data, symptoms, moods, and other health information are stored **locally on your device only**. This means:

- Your period logs remain private on your device
- No health data is uploaded to any server
- Your data is not accessible to us or anyone else
- You have complete control over your health information
- Data is not backed up to cloud services by the app

## Permissions

FemGlow may request the following permissions:

- **Notifications:** To remind you about upcoming periods, fertile windows, and medication reminders
- **Storage:** To store your period tracking data locally on your device

We only request permissions that are essential for the app's functionality.

## Third-Party Services

FemGlow does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising networks that may collect information used to identify you.

## Data Security

Since all your data is stored locally on your device:

- Your data security depends on your device's security
- We recommend using device lock features (PIN, password, fingerprint, face ID)
- If you uninstall the app, all data will be permanently deleted
- We do not have access to recover your data if it's lost

## Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Your Rights

Since we don't collect your data, you maintain complete control:

- All data belongs to you
- You can delete all data by uninstalling the app
- No data is retained by us after app deletion
- No data is shared with any third parties

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

- Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Notifying users through the app (if applicable)

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:

- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- Other applicable data protection laws
